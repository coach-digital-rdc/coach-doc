# Observations and people

## Observation sessions

Every session sent by the devices, filtered by area, by status (closed or in progress) and by function of the observer. Each row gives the date, the teacher, the school, the observer, the status, the average of the global scores and the appreciation. The **Export (Excel)** button downloads the filtered list.

A click on a session opens its sheet:

- the header: teacher, school, observer and function, class, average and appreciation (with the appreciation suggested by the application when the observer changed it);
- **Grid and scores**: the answer to each behaviour and the global score of each sub-domain;
- **Checklist**: the documents shown or not, with the proposals of the observer;
- **Notes**: the notes taken during the observation, with their time;
- **PDP**: the objectives validated with the teacher, their actions, indicators and due dates;
- **Signatures**: teacher, school head and observer, or the reason for the refusal or the absence;
- **Header and advice**: school year, weekly load, last observation, observed activity, times and advice.

The **Observation sheet** and **PDP** buttons open the print dialog of the browser with the document laid out as in the application; choose **Save as PDF** to get the file.

## Learning circles

The circles led by the observers: date, unit, school, observer, participants and rating of the participation.

## Exports

Three Excel files, limited to the chosen area:

| File | Sheets |
| --- | --- |
| Detailed results | Sessions (with the score of each sub-domain), Answers (one column per behaviour), Checklist, PDP objectives |
| List of sessions | Sessions |
| Learning circles | Circles |

## Observers

The observers with their function, duty station, the schools they follow, their number of sessions and their last session. A click opens the observer, where the name, the e-mail (which receives the login code of the application), the phone, the sex, the function, the duty station and the P.O. box shown on the observation sheet can be corrected. The **Add an observer** button creates an account.

The scope of an observer is the schools they chose in the application.

## Teachers

The teachers registered by the observers, with their school, subject, weekly load and observations. Their identity can be corrected from their sheet.

## Schools

The schools with their number and their Province, PROVED and Sub-PROVED. The administrators can add, import from a CSV file, edit or delete them. The ⋮ menu of a school makes the QR code that prepares a device for this school.

## Synchronisations

For the administrators: each device once, with its model, Android version, last synchronisation and number of synchronisations. A device that has not synchronised for 7 days or more is flagged, as in the application.
